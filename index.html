Questions you might have

1. Is this actually around the same price as Costco?

Yes. By cutting out middleman grocery stores and delivering straight to neighborhood drop spots, you pay wholesale farmer rates per pound plus a 20¢ per item fee. You buy exact quantities, so you don't pay for giant bulk packages that rot in your fridge.

2. What if I can't pick up my bag on time?

Bags are pre-packed with your name on them and held safely at your neighborhood drop spot throughout the pickup window on Mondays and Thursdays. If something comes up and you miss it, remaining produce goes straight to local food banks so nothing is wasted.
